Step 5 of 6

Complete one simple test trip

You do not need to go boating for this test. You can complete it safely from home.

  1. Open Marine Safe.
  2. Complete the profile setup.
  3. Select a launch ramp.
  4. Set the people on board.
  5. Set a return time shortly ahead.
  6. Start the trip.
  7. Check that the active-trip screen appears.
  8. Open Manage Trip.
  9. End the trip.
This is only a software test. Do not rely on Marine Safe as an emergency service or replacement for 000, marine radio or official marine rescue services.

What we want testers to check

You do not need to be technical. Just use Marine Safe like you would on a normal day on the water.

Trip setup

Is choosing the launch point, return time and starting a trip obvious?

On-water use

Can you quickly understand your trip status, ETA and what to do if plans change?

Safety gear

Does storing your boat, licence and safety-equipment information feel simple and useful?

How it works

A simple routine before you head out

Marine Safe keeps the focus on where you're leaving from, when you're due back and what to do when plans change.

1

Plan your trip

Choose your launch point and confirm the basics before leaving the ramp.

2

Set your return time

Tell Marine Safe when you expect to be back and start your trip.

3

Stay on top of the time

See your trip status and receive reminders as your return time approaches.

4

End the trip safely

When you're safely back, end the trip and close out the journey.
